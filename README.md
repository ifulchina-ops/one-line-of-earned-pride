One line, 1 to 240 characters, that gives the judge a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene. Free to enter, judged in pairs on genuine earned pride, a concrete scene, landing in one read and freshness.

The entry: after three years of pushing her soto cart out before dawn, Ibu Sari sells her last bowl before noon — and wipes the empty pot twice, just to be sure it was really empty. A small person doing something hard, a long effort finally landing, told in one read with no trophy, no podium, no graduation.

## The line

> Three years Ibu Sari pushed her soto cart out before dawn; today the last bowl sold before noon, and she wiped the empty pot twice, just to be sure it was really empty.

168 characters, one line.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
